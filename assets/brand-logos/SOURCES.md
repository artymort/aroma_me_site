# Brand logo sources

Brand wordmarks used to identify the corresponding perfume houses.
Sourced 2026-10-13 and 2026-10-14. Trademarks belong to their respective owners.
Where a usable SVG existed, its vector outlines were retained. Where only a
raster wordmark was available, the original mark was traced into SVG paths and
visually checked. The brand panels render all marks in black for consistency.
No brand wordmark is created from a site font.

For older or otherwise unavailable first-party vector marks, vector candidates
were checked against brand imagery and sourced from Wikimedia Commons,
WorldVectorLogo, or Seeklogo. These include Azzaro, Britney Spears, Bvlgari,
Burberry, Calvin Klein, Comme des Garcons, Ex Nihilo, Giorgio Armani, Granado,
Gucci, Guerlain, Hermes, Issey Miyake, Jean Paul Gaultier, L'Artisan Parfumeur,
Lalique, Lancome, Maison Alhambra, Maison Francis Kurkdjian, Mancera,
Marc Jacobs, Montblanc, Narciso Rodriguez, Natura, O Boticario,
Penhaligon's, Prada, Rabanne, Ralph Lauren, Van Cleef & Arpels, Versace,
and Victoria's Secret. Review each brand's trademark usage before public launch.

Standalone SVGs include the SVG namespace. Chanel's HTML-lowercased `viewbox`
is normalized to XML `viewBox`; Dolce&Gabbana's symbol is wrapped as a standalone SVG.
Creed's viewBox trims empty margins without altering the original outlines.
